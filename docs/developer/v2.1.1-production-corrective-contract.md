# v2.1.1 Production Corrective Contract

## Request assembly

`Request Builder` may receive either the legacy provider/template/recipient inputs or one embedded Provider Selector item containing `providerAllocation`, `invoiceTemplate`, `recipient`, and `job`. Missing physical n8n input indexes are treated as absent optional inputs only when n8n reports that index as unconnected.

## Odoo preflight

Preflight performs `common.version`, authentication, active-currency discovery, required-field `fields_get`, and read-only `search_count` probes. It does not call private or externally unavailable access-right methods and creates no customer, invoice, wizard, message, or mail record.


## Runtime rehydration

After every retry or failover Wait node, the canonical Odoo workflow reads the provider Sheet again and executes Provider Loader before provider selection. This reconstructs the process-local provider pool and secret vault after long waits, worker changes, or process restarts. Same-account retries re-enter Provider Selector with `requiredProfileId`; pre-side-effect failover preserves attempted-profile exclusion and selects another eligible profile.

## Exactly-once boundary

Campaign and job identity seed both idempotency and Odoo-visible references. A transport error during a potential side effect is reconciled against the same partner email or invoice reference. Cross-account failover is allowed only when the provider proves no side effect; an unresolved result becomes manual review.

## Ordered writeback

The production workflow persists job identity, writes `PROCESSING`, performs the provider operation, stages a durable writeback bundle, serially writes all managed tables, marks the bundle complete, and only then retries, fails over, or advances the recipient loop. Pending bundles are repaired before any new provider request.

## Campaign controller

Campaign correctness is reconstructed from `email_list`, `retry_queue`, `invoice_results`, and `campaign_report`. Process memory and workflow static data are caches only. Maximum jobs, terminal-failure threshold, inter-send delay, bulk confirmation, critical pause, and restart resume operate across the complete one-item loop.

One execution may process only one pending `Campaign_ID`. Before provider selection, the workflow acquires an `ACTIVE` campaign lease in `campaign_report`, rereads the row, and verifies the exact `Run_ID`, `Revision`, and unexpired `Lock_Expires_At`. The loop refreshes campaign revision/last-attempt evidence, and completion releases the same lease. Google Sheets is not transactional; concurrent manual starts for one campaign remain prohibited.

## Phase 04 capability and issuer correction

Preflight, sender, and tests use `shared/odoo/OdooCapabilityManifest.ts`. Only Odoo majors 18 and 19 are accepted; unknown majors fail closed before authentication. Odoo failover groups require `Issuer_Key` and matching authenticated company identity. Read-only preflight reports `CAPABILITY_VALIDATED_SIDE_EFFECT_PERMISSION_UNPROVEN`; live side-effect permission remains a canary gate.

## Phase 06 reporting correction

Campaign and account aggregate writes are compare-before-write operations. A candidate is valid only when the current Sheet revision equals its `Base_Revision` and its `Revision` is exactly one greater. Campaign candidates must also be owned by the active `Run_ID`. This is stale-writer protection, not a claim that Google Sheets provides database transactions; the same-campaign single-execution rule remains mandatory.

Startup rebuilds campaign totals from durable recipient/result/retry rows. The prior campaign row remains authoritative for lease, pause, and revision metadata but cannot inflate rebuilt counters. Account state starts from the highest-revision durable account row. Issuer mismatches are reported separately under `Campaign_ID=PREFLIGHT` and never routed to provider execution.

## Phase 07 release contract

Release approval requires the 126-node/141-edge canonical workflow to import/export successfully and the eight-node dry-run fixture to execute under exactly n8n 2.31.6, plus reviewed one-recipient canary and five-recipient/two-account pilot evidence. Mock Odoo fixtures prove adapter behavior but do not replace provider/inbox acceptance evidence.


## Final corrective operation recovery

The operation envelope is created after Request Builder so its `Stable_Reference` is the exact provider request reference. An unresolved `PROVIDER_PENDING` envelope becomes an explicit recovery run on startup. The original Profile_ID and stable reference are preserved, and Odoo reconciliation runs before any create/send side effect. The active campaign lease is reread and verified immediately before the envelope/sender path.
